espeak-ng is used by Ztoryc to turn words into PHONEMES for lip sync.

*** IMPORTANT — WHY IT IS A SEPARATE PROCESS ***

espeak-ng is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3.
Ztoryc NEVER links against it and never includes any of its code: it is
invoked as a SEPARATE COMMAND-LINE PROCESS, exactly as ffmpeg and Rhubarb are.
Linking it would impose the GPL on Ztoryc as a whole.

If a build of Ztoryc DISTRIBUTES an espeak-ng binary, the GPLv3 obligations
attach to that binary: the corresponding source must be offered to the
recipients. See LICENSE_espeak-ng.txt for the full licence text when a binary
is shipped.

espeak-ng source code can be found at:
https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng

When no espeak-ng is found, Ztoryc writes the WORDS in the lip sync column
instead of the phonemes: the feature degrades, it does not fail.
