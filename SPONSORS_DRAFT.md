```markdown
## 👋 Hi, I'm Franco — I build Ztoryc

**Ztoryc** is the first **open-source storyboard & animatic pipeline** built right
inside a 2D animation app (a fork of Tahoma2D). Draw your storyboard, time it into
an animatic with audio, track production, and hand off shots to animation — all in
one free tool, on macOS and Windows.

I'm a 2D animator and teacher: I build Ztoryc in the open so that students, indie
artists and small studios get a professional pre-production workflow **without
paying for proprietary software**.

### What your sponsorship funds
- 🚀 **New features** — storyboard, animatic, production tracking, Kitsu sync
- 🐛 **Bug fixes & stability** across macOS and Windows
- 📚 **Documentation & video tutorials** (the manual is already bilingual EN/IT)
- 🆓 **Keeping Ztoryc free and open-source**, forever

Every tier — even a coffee — directly buys development time. Studios and schools:
your logo can ride along in the README and on the site. **Thank you for making
open-source pre-production sustainable!** ❤️

*(IT) Ztoryc è la prima pipeline open-source di storyboard e animatic dentro
un'app di animazione 2D. Sostenerlo finanzia nuove feature, fix, documentazione e
lo tiene gratuito per sempre. Grazie!*
```
